> The M470D is the same as the M470A. http://wbsps.kuzub.com/modules/wbsps%20-%20module%20-%20m470d%20input%20module%20001.jpg shows the module. All you need to do is ignore the outputs for AUX 1/2 and the sub buss outputs (however i assume it used aux buses 1 and 3 on the 470A....but you wont use them when rack mounting them! so who cares) . If all you're trying to do is rack mount it... the inputs are exactly the same and the "M1 and M2" outputs are identical. Use the direct outs, same again. The M470 series of modules are all identical PCB boards with several components removed.
